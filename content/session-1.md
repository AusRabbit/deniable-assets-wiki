The crew were supposed to deliver [[vega-squad]], babysit the ship and be ready for extraction. Instead, they left with [[imperial-moff|the mission's target]] alive, a damaged freighter and Vega still on the ground.

## The support act

Using forged access codes and a landing arranged by an unnamed Alliance supporter, [[kryss-dorn]] brought the [[mynocks-mullet]] into a luxury [[luxury-spaceport|spaceport]] on an Imperial jungle world. His inverted approach ended in a neat touchdown and several decidedly unwell Vega operators. [[axel]] told the crew to stay put, then his squad disappeared through every exit except the obvious one.

[[voshkar]] rigged a log trap that looked more likely to hit the ship than an attacker. Kryss and [[tivik-ordo]] broke into the reception area's liquor supply and carried off a case of expensive booze. [[nira-dathwen]] found a perch above an entrance and settled in to watch.

## A very convenient emergency

[[armand]] arrived with six security droids and wanted an explanation. Kryss tried charm and introduced himself by his real name. Nira followed with a better story: they were a medical extraction team waiting for a casualty, urgently in need of fuel. An expired medical credential and a conveniently timed emergency alert sold the lie.

There was one problem. They were occupying the Moff's personal landing pad, and he was inbound. Every other berth was full. Armand wanted both the freighter and its crew gone, and the liquor returned.

Tivik found reports of violence at a nearby educational establishment, apparently where Vega had gone looking for the Moff. The crew decided to reach the target first. While Voshkar distracted Armand with an unsuccessful attempt to steal his pen and a successful bite to the hand, Nira prepared a sedative for the liquor. Propaganda material helped identify the target as a Chandrilan man. They returned the doctored bottles and lifted off; Voshkar's logs tumbled onto the pad behind them.

## Beneath the luxury

Before disconnecting from the port's systems, Tivik noticed repeated orders for enormous quantities of basic food, far beyond anything the reception area could need. With the Moff's Lambda shuttle now occupying their berth, the crew decided to investigate.

Kryss put the freighter down in a nearby street, crushing a speeder and damaging the left landing-gear assembly. All four entered through the public lobby and took a lift below the polished reception areas. They found military barracks and a sizable Imperial garrison: the Moff's extraction force. The food mystery had a much less profitable answer than they had hoped.

Tivik stayed behind to rig three charges around the lift access routes. The others went to Docking Bay Four to check on their patient.

## The bedside manner wears off

The sedative had worked. The Moff lay unconscious amid anxious attendants, and Armand was relieved to see the supposed medical team return. Voshkar began dragging the patient by the ankles while Nira insisted on an immediate evacuation. [[lieutenant-voight]] refused to let them take him. This time, the medical story did not hold.

Voight opened fire on Kryss. He killed the two stormtroopers guarding the scene, and his shots damaged an electrical panel, temporarily disrupting one group of security droids. Voshkar lunged for Armand and was knocked away with a datapad. Nira answered with a surgical vibroscalpel, badly wounding the deputy inspector and using him as a shield.

Tivik arrived after finishing the charges and destroyed a group of three droids with a frag grenade. Voshkar's next attack failed to injure Voight, but his filthy distraction left her prone. Kryss damaged the remaining droid group, then took return fire that left him wounded and with scattered senses. Nira's final attack put Armand out of the fight, bleeding heavily; nobody stopped to establish whether he would survive.

## Site B

The charges detonated as the garrison responded. With alarms sounding and Voight and surviving droids still a threat, the crew made a fighting withdrawal. Voshkar hauled the Moff out by a rope around his legs while Kryss covered the retreat. The demolished lift routes bought time, but troops began emerging from other doors.

Nira called Axel, announced that they had the package and told Vega to use Site B, then ended the transmission amid furious protests. No pickup or alternate rendezvous was agreed. Tivik's vote of no confidence in Kryss's parking put him in the pilot's seat for departure, with Kryss relegated to co-pilot.

Tivik got the freighter airborne under fire. The crushed speeder remained caught on a landing leg long enough to strike nearby buildings, then fell among pursuing droids. The crew escaped the spaceport with the Moff alive. Vega stayed behind.

## Where things stand

- The Moff is aboard; his destination, handover and what to do when he wakes remain unresolved.
- Vega's extraction and the consequences of abandoning [[axel]] are still open.
- The freighter's damaged landing gear needs attention.
- Kryss was wounded and suffered a critical injury; lasting recovery is not established in the recording. Nira also took blaster fire.
- Armand was left incapacitated and bleeding. Voight and some security droids remained active during the withdrawal.
