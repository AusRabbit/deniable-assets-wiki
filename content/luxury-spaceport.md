A ground-based spaceport on an unnamed Imperial jungle world, visited in [[session-1]]. Neither the world's name nor the port's proper name is established clearly in the recording.

## Layout

Around ten to twelve landing pads form a ring around a central botanical garden. The public lobby operates around the clock, with lifts leading to lower levels. Docking Bay Four is [[imperial-moff|the Moff]]'s private berth, with an expensive reception area and liquor supply. [[armand]] oversees preparations there.

## Beneath the port

[[tivik-ordo]] noticed repeated orders for unusually large quantities of cheap food. The crew investigated and discovered a sizable military garrison beneath the luxury facilities, assigned to support the Moff's extraction. This explained the supplies; the speculation about smuggling was never confirmed.

## After the raid

Tivik rigged three charges around lift access routes. They detonated when the garrison responded to the firefight, delaying pursuit without sealing every exit. Troops emerged through other doors as the crew fled. The Moff was taken alive, the reception area became a battlefield, and the [[mynocks-mullet]] escaped from a nearby street after damaging its landing gear on a speeder.
