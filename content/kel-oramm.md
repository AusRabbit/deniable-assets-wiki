A Mon Calamari major in Rebel Intelligence and the crew's semi-regular handler. He expects to be called "sir" and is hard to read until he isn't.

## Session 2

He debriefed the crew aboard the [[rebel-frigate|Mon Calamari frigate]] after the Moff job. He wanted to know where [[vega-squad]] was. When [[nira-dathwen]] explained the B-Site arrangement, he threw a datapad at her and spent half an hour berating the crew. This was not the first time they had left Vega behind.

He docked their pay and put a moratorium on repairing the [[mynocks-mullet]] at the Rebellion's expense. He did not notice [[tivik-ordo]] pocketing the datapad he had thrown, a sensitive intelligence briefing.

His attendant later gave the crew their next assignment: bring in the slicer [[feeler]] on [[brentaal-iv]].
