A human bounty hunter and assassin from Coruscant: professional, quiet and precise, built around one job — putting a shot exactly where it needs to go, at whatever range the moment calls for. He trusts his rifle more than any client.

## Kit

A DDC MR6 modular rifle that switches between a scoped long rifle and a handier carbine, backed up by an SE-14C blaster pistol. Armoured clothing, sporting macrobinoculars, a spare reload and a pair of stimpacks. Quick on the draw and quick to react.

## History

- Has applied to the [[rebel-alliance]] three times and been rejected three times. Still thinks he's Tier 1 material.
- Has an open invitation from the [[galactic-empire]] to join its commando ranks, sitting unanswered in his inbox.

**Obligation:** Betrayal.

**Critical injuries:** Scattered Senses (Session 1). Treated, with no ongoing effect, but it still counts; the next critical injury adds +10.

## Session 1

Piloted the arrival at the [[luxury-spaceport|luxury spaceport]], then damaged the ship's left landing gear while parking on a speeder in the street. In the firefight he killed two stormtroopers and disrupted security droids, but took blaster wounds and a scattered-senses critical injury. [[tivik-ordo]] took over for the escape. No completed treatment of the critical injury is established.

## Session 2

Gave the debrief, reporting the Moff in the hold and proposing that repairs go on Rebel insurance. Nira treated his critical injury on the [[rebel-frigate|frigate]]; it stays on his record, but its effects are gone. He secured Tivik's explosives discount by slamming a pistol on the quartermaster's counter.

On [[brentaal-iv]], he paid generously at the bar and drove off two thugs with autofire. He froze calmly on a pressure-plate trap in the [[droid-factory|droid factory]] and exposed its wiring for Tivik. Against [[hush-and-whisper]], he took a shock-claw hit, stunned one droid point-blank and destroyed the other with a rifle shot that overloaded a panel. He came out of the fight heavily wounded and was patched up with a medpac, a stim and a death stick.

**Picked up:** a packet of death sticks, 50 credits and a small bag of unidentified white powder, dropped by a fleeing thug.

**Flag:** wants a long-range assassin's shot — the kind of cinematic sniper moment his rifle was built for.
