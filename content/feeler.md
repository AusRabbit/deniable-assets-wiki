A slicer who goes by many handles; Feeler is the most common. According to Rebel Intelligence, Feeler's data packet supplied the intelligence, forged credentials and landing information that put the crew on [[imperial-moff|the Moff]]'s private pad in [[session-1]]. Then Feeler went incommunicado.

Rebel Intelligence finds the arrangement suspicious and wants Feeler brought in. In [[session-2]], the crew traced the last data packet to a [[droid-factory|derelict droid factory]] on [[brentaal-iv]]. The factory had been rewired with fresh data cabling and guarded by traps and two improvised lurker droids, [[hush-and-whisper]], both built by Feeler.

According to Hush, Feeler had stepped away to attend a function. Where and what that function is remains to be found in the factory's intelligence.
