The catalogue is the campaign's Genesys talent list, revised on 18 October 2026 for Star Wars play:

- **Magic is out.** 47 spell-casting and other magic talents from the fantasy Genesys settings have been removed.
- **The Force is in.** 125 Force talents from *Star Wars Genesys* (DrainSmith) have been added: the Force power trees (Bind, Enhance, Force Leap, Heal/Harm, Influence, Misdirect, Move, Protect/Unleash and Sense), the lightsaber forms, and Force Sensitive and Force Rating. A **Force** badge marks them. Only a Force Sensitive character can buy a Force talent.
- **More Star Wars talents.** 33 other Star Wars talents are new too, covering droids, jetpacks, stims, clones and lightsaber combat.
- **Reflect has two versions.** *Reflect* is the shield or melee-weapon talent. *Reflect (Lightsaber)* is the lightsaber one. Each card says which it is.

Talents cost tier × 5 XP whichever version or source they come from. Rules text uses the narrative-dice symbols; open **Symbol key** below for what each one means. Use the controls to narrow the list. Ordinary text search also matches rules text, notes and source abbreviations.
