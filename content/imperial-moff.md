A Chandrilan Imperial Moff and the target of the operation in [[session-1]]. His personal name is not clear enough in the recording to establish a canonical spelling.

He arrived by Lambda shuttle at his private berth in the [[luxury-spaceport|luxury spaceport]], supported by attendants, [[lieutenant-voight]], stormtroopers and a garrison housed beneath the port. [[armand]] had been preparing the landing pad for him.

## Capture

[[nira-dathwen]] sedated him through doctored liquor, then attempted to remove him under cover of a medical evacuation. When that failed, the crew fought their way out. [[voshkar]] dragged him to the [[mynocks-mullet]], and he survived the extraction.

At the end of Session 1 he was alive aboard the crew's ship.

## Session 2

The crew brought him to the [[rebel-frigate|Rebel frigate]]. Rebel examination found a fractured skull, which [[nira-dathwen]] attributed to "a bit of a fall"; she described the induced coma as proactive. He appears to have passed into Rebel hands, though no formal handover was narrated. What the Alliance intends to do with him is unknown.
