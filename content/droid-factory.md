A decrepit warehouse on [[brentaal-iv]], behind fencing and open ground, across the street from a third-shift bar. It once manufactured droids. The production line is long dead, leaving a nest of conveyors, dangling armatures, spare limbs and half-finished protocol droids.

It hasn't operated for a couple of cycles, but the bartender across the street said a crew had recently been paid well to install data cabling inside. Rebel Intelligence places [[feeler]]'s last transmission here.

## Defences

There are no cameras, patrols or turrets outside; the dilapidation is the camouflage. Inside, [[kryss-dorn]] found a pressure plate wired into the building's power, which [[tivik-ordo]] disarmed by slicing its control terminal. [[nira-dathwen]] triggered a ceiling net made from repurposed cargo mesh. Two lurker droids, [[hush-and-whisper]], ambushed the crew.

## After the fight

A frag grenade left the gantry to the upper-floor offices hanging askew. Whisper was destroyed; Hush shut down after Tivik questioned it. The crew holds the building, and the GM has said it contains enough intelligence to identify Feeler's next move. It's unknown whether the firefight was noticed outside.
