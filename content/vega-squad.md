An elite squad led by [[axel]], also called Vega Company. The crew of the [[mynocks-mullet]] serves as its transport and support detail, receives limited briefing and gets little respect in return. [[nira-dathwen]] knows Axel's medical history and that of his not-so-faithful wife.

## Session 1

Vega disembarked at the [[luxury-spaceport|luxury spaceport]] and headed into the city on an operation targeting [[imperial-moff|an Imperial Moff]]. Reports and visible fires placed their apparent activity at a nearby educational establishment while the Moff arrived at the port instead.

The crew captured the Moff and left without collecting Vega. Nira told Axel to go to Site B, but no alternate rendezvous or later pickup was agreed. Vega's extraction remains unresolved.

## Session 2

Major [[kel-oramm|Oramm]] was furious to hear Vega had been left behind and told the crew it was not the first time. Rebel command expects Vega Company to "surface" on its own. [[nira-dathwen]] invoked doctor–patient confidentiality and hinted at Axel's medical history.
