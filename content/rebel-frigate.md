A sizeable Mon Calamari frigate of the [[rebel-alliance]], keeping station over a nondescript asteroid belt in the Mid Rim. The crew reports there between missions. Its name hasn't been given.

- **Briefing room** — a Kashyyyk-wood table where [[kel-oramm|Major Oramm]] conducts debriefs, at volume.
- **Medical bay** — well equipped; the crew recovers here between jobs. [[nira-dathwen]] found an organ cooler full of ice cream.
- **Quartermaster** — a cage behind chain-link fencing with a queue of people waiting for equipment. Gear, including better armour, can be negotiated for here between missions. [[tivik-ordo]] owes him 200 credits.

## Session 2

The crew were debriefed after the Moff job, patched up and given their next mission. [[voshkar]] was pulled aside for random drug and alcohol testing before departure and did not leave with them. The [[mynocks-mullet]] left without the repairs it had requisitioned.
