A human Imperial lieutenant accompanying [[imperial-moff|the Moff]] at the [[luxury-spaceport]] in [[session-1]]. The transcript most often renders her name as Voight; its spelling remains provisional.

She refused to let the crew take the unconscious Moff and opened fire when [[nira-dathwen]]'s deception failed. She concentrated on [[kryss-dorn]], recognising the threat posed by his rifle, and wounded him.

[[voshkar]] knocked her prone through a distraction, but did not defeat her. She was still firing during the crew's withdrawal. Her next move is unknown.
