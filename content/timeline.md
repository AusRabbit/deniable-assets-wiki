## Session 1

- `M01` **A stylish arrival** — [[kryss-dorn]] lands the [[mynocks-mullet]] at the [[luxury-spaceport|luxury spaceport]]. [[axel]] leads [[vega-squad]] away, leaving the crew on standby.
- `M01` **Medical cover** — [[armand]] confronts the crew. [[nira-dathwen]] convinces him they are medical responders, but he orders them off the Moff's private pad.
- `M01` **Return the booze** — Nira prepares a sedative, [[voshkar]] distracts Armand, and the crew returns the doctored liquor before lifting off.
- `M01` **Bad parking, good lead** — Following [[tivik-ordo]]'s discovery of excessive food orders, Kryss lands in the street, crushing a speeder and damaging the landing gear.
- `M01` **The garrison below** — The crew discovers the Moff's extraction force beneath the port. Tivik rigs three charges around its lift access routes.
- `M01` **The bluff breaks** — The crew finds [[imperial-moff|the Moff]] unconscious. [[lieutenant-voight]] blocks the attempted medical evacuation, triggering a firefight.
- `M01` **Fighting withdrawal** — Stormtroopers and several droids fall; Armand is left bleeding and incapacitated. Kryss is injured. Tivik's charges detonate as the crew drags the Moff out.
- `M01` **Vega left behind** — Nira directs Axel to Site B without arranging a pickup. Tivik flies the damaged freighter away with the live Moff aboard and Vega still on the ground.

## Session 2

- `INT` **The debrief** — Aboard the [[rebel-frigate|Rebel frigate]], [[kel-oramm|Major Oramm]] learns Vega was left behind and that the Moff has a fractured skull. He docks the crew's pay and refuses repairs. [[tivik-ordo]] pockets his thrown datapad.
- `INT` **A new assignment** — Rebel Intelligence wants the slicer [[feeler]], who arranged the crew's landing at the Moff's pad, brought in from [[brentaal-iv]].
- `INT` **Requisitions** — [[kryss-dorn]]'s pistol on the counter gets Tivik a case of explosives for 200 credits. [[voshkar]] is held back for drug and alcohol testing.
- `M02` **Asking for Feeler** — At a shift workers' bar, the bartender reveals that new data cabling was recently installed in a derelict warehouse nearby. Two thugs try to rob [[nira-dathwen]] outside and are driven off.
- `M02` **Into the droid factory** — Kryss freezes on a pressure-plate mine that Tivik disarms. Nira is caught under a ceiling net.
- `M02` **Hush and Whisper** — Two lurker droids ambush the crew. Whisper is destroyed; Hush is stunned and sliced, revealing that Feeler built them and is away at a function.
