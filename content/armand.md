A meticulously dressed bureaucrat responsible for clearing [[imperial-moff|the Moff]]'s private landing pad at the [[luxury-spaceport]]. He carries a datapad, a pistol and a pen that [[voshkar]] badly wanted.

## Session 1

Armand confronted the crew with six security droids. [[nira-dathwen]] convinced him they were medical responders, but he still required them to move the ship and return the liquor. He accepted the returned case without discovering the sedative.

When the crew returned to collect the unconscious Moff, Armand initially welcomed their help. The attempted extraction became a firefight. He knocked Voshkar away with his datapad, then was severely wounded by Nira's vibroscalpel. He was left incapacitated and bleeding; his eventual survival is unconfirmed.
