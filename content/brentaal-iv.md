A densely built city world, visited in [[session-2]]. It is nowhere near Coruscant, but its cityscape crowds every horizon. Imperials are active here, and where they go an underbelly follows: plenty of places for slicers to operate.

Rebel Intelligence traced [[feeler]]'s last data packet to a [[droid-factory|derelict droid factory]] here. The nearby bars run on shift work, serving third-, fourth- and fifth-shift crews in hi-vis.
