A Duros technician and droid tech: slicer, mechanic and demolitions support. A professional who thinks the job is the work, not the spotlight. Like most Duros, he has a feel for astrogation.

He is not built for sneaking, though — a real gap for a crew that may need to be smuggled in quietly.

## Kit

Slicer gear and a datapad, a demolitions tool kit, three Destro-6 breaching charges and two frag grenades. An IR-5 blaster pistol with a filed front sight. Armoured clothing, a comlink and two stimpacks.

## History

- His slicer handle is on both the [[rebel-alliance]] and [[galactic-empire]] watchlists for questionable holonet activity.
- Took in [[nira-dathwen]] when she had nowhere to go, giving her work and a place to stay. (Hook from Session 2.)
- Is currently blackmailing Alliance and Imperial officials in a concurrent scam. Neither side can find out.

**Obligation:** Blackmail.

## Session 1

Accessed the [[luxury-spaceport|luxury spaceport]]'s systems and discovered the food orders that led the crew to its hidden garrison. Rigged three charges around the lift routes, destroyed three security droids with a grenade, and flew the damaged [[mynocks-mullet]] out with [[imperial-moff|the Moff]] aboard. The charges detonated during the withdrawal. Explosive supplies need checking before the next job; the final escape narration does not establish a reliable remaining count.

## Session 2

Pocketed the datapad [[kel-oramm|Major Oramm]] threw during the debrief. It is a sensitive intelligence briefing, and its contents are not yet revealed. He haggled with the quartermaster for a ready-packed case of grenades and charges and owes him 200 credits.

On [[brentaal-iv]], he went by his mission codename, Jaxon Kel. He disarmed a pressure-plate mine by slicing its terminal, spotted the lurker droids first, threw a frag grenade at the gantry and pinned [[hush-and-whisper|Whisper]] with pistol fire. He then sliced Hush and learned that [[feeler]] built both droids and had left to attend a function.

His obligation came up at the end of Session 1 but did not trigger in Session 2. The GM is carrying it into Session 3.
