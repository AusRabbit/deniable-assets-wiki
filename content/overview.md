A new Star Wars campaign. Nothing has happened yet: the four operatives haven't met the story, and they may not have met each other. Who hires them, what the job is and how they end up in the same room are all for Session 1 to decide.

What they do have is a skill set that adds up to a crew, a ship built to be forgotten, and a lot of history with both sides of the war.

## The Crew

- [[kryss-dorn]] — Human bounty hunter and assassin. The marksman.
- [[voshkar]] — Trandoshan enforcer. The one who stands in the doorway.
- [[nira-dathwen]] — Bothan doctor and social operator, with credentials that expired a while ago.
- [[tivik-ordo]] — Duros slicer and demolitions tech, wanted by both sides for his holonet habits.

## The Ship

The [[mynocks-mullet]], a battered light freighter with mismatched plating and a scrubbed registry — one of thousands like it in the Outer Rim.

## Both Sides of the War

Every member of the crew is tangled up with both the [[galactic-empire]] and the [[rebel-alliance]]. [[kryss-dorn]] keeps applying to the Rebels and keeps getting an Imperial offer instead. [[voshkar]] trains clients from both. [[tivik-ordo]] is on both sides' watchlists and is blackmailing officials in each. [[nira-dathwen]] can no longer practise in the Core Worlds at all. See [[threads]].

## How We Play

- Players roll all of their own characters' checks. The GM rolls NPC checks openly and never rolls for a player character.
- The difficulty of a check is stated before you roll, and it depends on the actual stakes rather than a fixed default.
- Declare what you want to do in your own words. There's no numbered menu of options.
- Initiative slots aren't assigned to specific characters; anyone eligible on that side can take one.
