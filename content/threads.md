### Tivik's scam
`hot` Obligation carried over

[[tivik-ordo]] is blackmailing officials in both the [[rebel-alliance]] and the [[galactic-empire]] at the same time, while his slicer handle sits on both sides' watchlists. His obligation came up at the end of Session 1, and one of his scams is coming to a head. It didn't trigger in Session 2; the GM has carried it into Session 3.

### Vega left behind
`open` Expected to surface

### A damaged getaway ship

The [[mynocks-mullet]]'s left landing-gear assembly was damaged when [[kryss-dorn]] landed on a speeder. [[kel-oramm|Major Oramm]] has refused to repair it at the Rebellion's expense, and the crew's pay is docked.
